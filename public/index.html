Broken Python

Eat the right keywords. Fix the code. Dodge the bug.

How to Play

  • Slither through the grid and eat Python keywords to fill the blanks in the console.
  • Green tiles are real keywords. Red tiles are imposters.
  • Words must be eaten in order. Wrong bite? Hit the top wall (the console) to spit it back out.
  • Don't crash into walls, yourself, or the rogue snake on the last level.
  • Arrows or WASD to move. Swipe or use the d-pad on mobile.
  • P / Esc pauses. M mutes.
